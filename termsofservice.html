Terms of Service

Quality Disclaimer



Suits can have asymmetric parts or imperfections. You can expect some imperfections in fur symmetry and gluing. We consider defects to be any visible rips, popped seams, or elements that affect the wearer’s comfort. We do our best to ensure that you are satisfied with your suit. ​

Report any fit issues. We are happy to discuss any fit issues. A lot of the time these are caused by errors in the duct tape dummy. We ask that customers contact us within 30 days of receiving their item to report any issues. ​

How to Commission

​

You must be 18+ to commission or an adult approval who is 18 or older. We can ask for an ID if we think it may be necessary, with the ID number covered if you prefer. If you are not 18, your tutor or legal guardian must do the purchase for you.

We will only work on original characters; we do not work on characters from Anime/Manga/Cartoons/Video Games/Movies. We also can’t make a fursuit from a character that is not owned by the client. But we can make hybrids or designs inspired by existing characters! ​

We have our style, so we cannot copy another artist’s work. We won’t accept requests about changing our style, like eye shape and iris style, or pickable nostrils. Discussion of muzzle shapes or any other preference is acceptable. ​

We are experienced in airbrushing but would only recommend it for shading details. ​

Commissions open through an application form. By applying, you will automatically accept our Terms of Service. Be sure to add all the notes, details, and preferences in the form! This will help us to be as accurate and organized on your commission as much as possible. ​

Once the request is sent, you could be selected from the submissions and receive an email with the quote of your commission. You can now accept it by sending a confirmation email within 48 hours. If no confirmation is sent, your commission will be denied, and another commissioner will be selected from the submissions. ​

If you decide to accept the quote, we can proceed with the next step. To reserve the slot, you need to pay the first 30% down within 72 hours. We can discuss it if you need more time for it. ​

Once the retainer fee is paid, you will be officially inserted in our Trello queue. Before starting your commission, we will send you samples of the fur we feel goes best with your reference sheet and then finalize the colors for purchase. Keep in mind that it’s not always possible to find the exact fur color of your reference. You will receive a little scheme with all the materials positioned on your character. In this step, you can still change some options or markings. Please keep in mind that changes are possible only before the project is started. For any changes requested after that time, there will be an added fee. ​

We will not accept a commission if the design of the character is stolen. We are not responsible if we don’t recognize a stolen design, since we can’t be aware of every single existing design.

Reference Sheets

​

Your reference should include: ​​

Front and back views of your character (side views are great too, but not required)+ tongue color. Please keep this in mind when you ask for a quote because we will ignore every quote request that doesn't include a ref with those characteristics!! Be sure that your reference is as accurate as possible; we don’t want to struggle to understand how some parts are made during the construction process. This will help us be quicker and more efficient. ​​

All markings should be shown very clearly, and they should be EXACTLY how you want them to look on your suit. We count every spot and stripe on a character to make our prices. So be sure to specify on the reference sheet or on the notes\suit preferences. If the spots number is casual or if you want an exact number of them. Keep in mind that spots, stripes, and complicated markings will increase the price of the suit. We prefer ref sheets without shades. ​​

​

DTD and Measurements:

​​

If you have commissioned a bodysuit from us, we will need a proper Duct Tape Dummy. This is a crucial element in ensuring your costume fits properly, so be sure to follow the tutorials and make it perfect! We will NOT make bodysuits without a proper duct tape dummy. We are not responsible if your DTD is not made properly and the costume won't fit you perfectly! ​​

We require that at least one of your feet be taped up. Your toes do not have to be included, but please tape up the heel and ankle. The foot must be attached to the leg of the full duct tape dummy; please do not send a foot separately. Remember to sign where your elbows and knees are! ​​

Tape your DTD back up completely before shipping it to us. Write your name and character name on the front of your DTD. Be sure to sign the position of your elbows, knees, and the tail position with a marker. Other measurements will be required on the application form if needed (head circumference, feet number, chest circumference, etc) ​ ​​

The process of working is decided by us. We will try to finish your commission as soon as possible, but if you need the commission for a certain date, it must be commissioned in time, and we will let you know if it is possible. ​​

Things that could delay your Turn Around Time:

Buyer is responsible for proper care of their fursuit:

  • Do not pick up head by ears, nose, or eyes
  • Brush the fur regularly
  • Wear a balaclava and an Under Armour to prevent build-up of sweat inside parts
  • Wash head and bodysuit consistently after every major use
  • Avoid mud, wine, coffee, or anything else known to stain.
  • Store in a cool and dry location; avoid direct sunlight and humidity.
  • Do not store for a long period of time unwashed.
  • Always use a fursuit spray after every use
  • Do not remove or modify parts.
  • Do not expose to heat (like leaving outdoors or in a hot car)
  • Hang dry only
​​

We also provide a fursuit care guide in your package. You can find these instructions here too. Buyer understands that fursuits are bulky, claustrophobic, hot, and have less visibility than normal eyesight. We are not responsible for any injury you may incur while in suit. ​​

Including, but not limited to: fatigue, overheating, heat stroke, falls, dizziness, panic due to claustrophobia. Please take care of yourself in suit! Take breaks, drink water, and have a friend be your handler in unfamiliar territory. ​​
