FAQ

What is a fursuit?
A fursuit is a costume that resembles an animal or anthropomorphic character, often made from faux fur, with a head, body, and sometimes additional features like tails, paws, and accessories. These suits are commonly worn at conventions, meet-ups, or for personal enjoyment.
How do I place an order for a fursuit?
To place an order, simply fill out of our Commission Form or our contact page. Be sure to include details about your character design, any specific features, and your size requirements.
What is the typical turnaround time for a fursuit?
The construction process takes from 1 to 6 months; this depends on your position in the queue. Once started, we will take a month or so to complete a head or a partial, and a month to 6 months for the full suit. We will not work with deadlines.
Can I provide my own materials or specific colors
We do NOT accept fur already bought from you or others. If you need a particular color from a seller you know, we can buy it from them, but this is not always possible (This could charge more than the total price).You can choose the length of the fur we should use for your project. If you don’t have preferences, we will suggest which is the best option for us, based on your character species or design.
What size do I need to order?
If you have commissioned a bodysuit from us, we will need a proper Duct Tape Dummy. This is a crucial element in ensuring your costume fits properly, so be sure to follow the tutorials and make it perfect! We will NOT make bodysuits without a proper duct tape dummy. We are not responsible if your DTD is not made properly and the costume won't fit you perfectly!
How do I care for my fursuit?
To maintain your fursuit, we recommend spot cleaning with a damp cloth and mild detergent. Avoid machine washing or drying, as this can damage the materials. Regularly brushing the faux fur will help keep it looking fresh.
Will my fursuit be customizable?
Yes! Each fursuit is made to match your vision. You can discuss customization options like markings, colors, and features with us to ensure it represents your character perfectly.
Do you accept payment plans?
Customers can choose to set up a payment plan with a minimum of 2 payments and a maximum of 6 payments. If you need a longer payment plan, we can discuss that option. To begin, we require a down payment of 30% of the total cost of your commission. After the initial payment, you can make monthly installments until the balance is paid off. You will have the flexibility to decide the date for your monthly payments. We allow a grace period of up to 2 weeks without requiring communication. However, after this period, we will charge a late fee of 3% of the total amount for each week of delay, or we may opt to cancel your commission without refunds. Therefore, it is important to communicate with us if you are unable to make a payment on the agreed date. We are willing to work with you on any issues that may arise.

If you need to postpone payments for any reason, please contact us directly to discuss the changes.
What if I have questions during the process?
We are here to help! Don’t hesitate to reach out with any questions or concerns during the creation process. We strive to respond to all inquiries as quickly as possible.
What is your return policy?
Please ensure that you genuinely want the commission and that it fits within your budget before making a purchase. Refunds are only available for fursuits that are not currently in production. Once the fur purchase is made, we can no longer accept refunds. If the commission is canceled, no fur or incomplete parts will be shipped to the client. Please note that we do not accept returns.
